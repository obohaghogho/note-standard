# NoteStandard — Video #01 Campaign Production Documentation

## Campaign Overview
- **Brand**: NoteStandard
- **Website**: www.notestandard.com
- **Product**: Multi-currency Digital Wallet Platform
- **Target Currencies**: NGN (Nigerian Naira 🇳🇬), USD (US Dollar 🇺🇸), GHS (Ghanaian Cedi 🇬🇭)
- **Target Audience**: Nigeria, Ghana, West Africa, International Users & Founders
- **Primary Platforms**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels (9:16 Vertical Video)

---

## 1. Master Voiceover Script & Audio Specification

### Exact Master Script
> "I built a fintech app here in Nigeria with NGN, US Dollars and Ghanaian Cedi in one wallet. Three currencies, with tools to deposit, withdraw, send and convert. This is NoteStandard. Would you use it?"

- **Tone**: Natural, confident, founder-like, warm, professional.
- **Audio Output**: 192kbps AAC stereo audio track.
