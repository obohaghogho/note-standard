## 3. KEY CLAUSES REQUIRING LEGAL COUNSEL REVIEW

### 3.1 The Regulatory Warranty Clause (`LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE EXECUTION`)
- **Clause Text:** Aggregator warrants that it *"has the required licenses and regulatory approvals to conduct its business and participate in this transaction."*
- **Forensic Finding:** NoteStandard currently possesses CAC registration (RC 9586407), but does **not** possess a direct SEC VASP licence or SCUML certificate `[VERIFIED]`.
- **Contractual Risk:** Signing the agreement containing this blanket warranty without a formal Legal Opinion confirming NoteStandard's regulatory status represents a material contractual and regulatory risk requiring legal review `[LEGAL DETERMINATION REQUIRED]`.
- **Action Required:** Counsel must negotiate an amendment to this clause (e.g. limiting the warranty to valid corporate incorporation and compliance with applicable laws as a non-custodial technology provider) OR issue a formal legal opinion confirming regulatory status `[LEGAL DETERMINATION REQUIRED]`.

### 3.2 LCIA London Arbitration Clause (`LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`)
- **Clause Text:** Disputes are subject to resolution under LCIA arbitration rules in London.
- **Legal Risk:** Arbitrating disputes in London under English/LCIA procedural rules imposes high legal costs on a Nigerian fintech operating in Effurun/Lagos.
- **Action Required:** Counsel should request amending dispute resolution to local Nigerian arbitration (e.g. Arbitration Law of Lagos State / Lagos Court of Arbitration) `[LEGAL DETERMINATION REQUIRED]`.
