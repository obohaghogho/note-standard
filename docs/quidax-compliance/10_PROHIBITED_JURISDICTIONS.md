## 4. NOTESTANDARD ENFORCEMENT MECHANISMS `[VERIFIED]` / `[INTERNAL DESIGN]`

NoteStandard enforces prohibited jurisdiction restrictions through three automated layers:

1. **Geographic IP Blocking:** Real-time IP address geolocation checks block web and app access originating from IP addresses assigned to Schedule 1 countries or the United States `[INTERNAL DESIGN]`.
2. **Identity Document Screening:** Prembly API checks automatically reject National IDs, passports, or driver's licences issued by Schedule 1 countries or the United States `[VERIFIED]`.
3. **User Attestation & Proof of Address:** Mandatory user attestation during Tier 1 sign-up confirming Nigerian residence, combined with Tier 3 utility bill verification for high-value accounts `[VERIFIED]`.
